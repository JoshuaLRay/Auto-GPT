# Dustin Yelton — Business & Launch Roadmap

A practical, ordered checklist to take the business from idea to "open for
calls." Some items have legal/tax deadlines, so the **order matters**.

> ⚠️ **Verify the specifics.** Fees, forms, and deadlines below were accurate
> as general guidance but change over time and vary by county. Confirm each
> dollar amount and deadline against the official source linked before relying
> on it. Nothing here is legal or tax advice.

---

## Phase A — Set up the business entity (do first; some clocks start here)

1. **Pick the legal structure.** A single-member **LLC** is the common choice
   for a one-person trade business — it separates personal and business
   liability and is cheap to maintain. (Talk to a CPA if revenue will be
   significant; an S-corp election can save self-employment tax later.)
2. **File the LLC with the Kentucky Secretary of State.**
   - Online at the KY SOS One Stop Business Portal: <https://onestop.ky.gov>
   - Articles of Organization filing fee is modest (verify current amount —
     historically around $40).
   - Pick the business name here (e.g. "Dustin Yelton Painting & Cleaning LLC")
     and confirm it's available in the state registry first.
3. **Get an EIN (federal tax ID).** Free, takes minutes, directly from the IRS
   — never pay a third party for this: <https://www.irs.gov/ein>
4. **Open a business bank account** using the LLC docs + EIN. Keep business and
   personal money separate from day one — it's what makes the LLC protection
   real and makes taxes sane.

## Phase B — Local & tax registration (time-sensitive)

5. **Kentucky tax registration.** Register through the KY One Stop portal for
   any state tax accounts you need (e.g. if you'll have employees or owe sales
   tax on certain services — most painting labor isn't taxed, but verify).
6. **County / city occupational license.** Northern Kentucky counties and
   cities require an **occupational license / business registration**, often
   with a **short deadline after you start doing business** (your earlier
   research noted ~10 days for Pendleton County). Check each place you'll
   operate:
   - **Pendleton County** — contact the County Clerk / occupational tax office.
   - **Campbell, Kenton, Bracken counties** — each has its own registration;
     register where you have a base of operations and where you regularly work.
   - Call the county offices directly to confirm forms, fees, and the deadline
     clock so you don't get penalized.

## Phase C — Insurance & certifications (do before taking paid work)

7. **General liability insurance.** Essential for a trades business — covers
   property damage and injury claims. Get 2–3 quotes; expect a small annual or
   monthly premium for a solo operator. This is also what lets you legitimately
   say "Insured" on the website.
8. **EPA Lead-Safe (RRP) certification.** *Federal requirement* for any
   renovation/painting that disturbs paint in homes/child-care facilities built
   **before 1978** (common in Northern Kentucky):
   - The **firm** must be EPA-certified as a Lead-Safe Certified Firm (apply via
     EPA, small fee, multi-year).
   - At least one person must be a **Certified Renovator** — an ~8-hour
     accredited training course.
   - Start: <https://www.epa.gov/lead/lead-renovation-repair-and-painting-program>
   - ⚠️ Don't put "EPA Lead-Safe Certified" on the website until the firm cert
     is actually issued.
9. **Workers' comp** — only if/when he hires employees (not needed for a true
   solo owner, but verify KY thresholds before bringing on help).

## Phase D — Get found online (highest ROI for local services)

10. **Google Business Profile (free, do this early).**
    <https://www.google.com/business> — for local services this often drives
    *more calls than the website itself.* Use the exact same business name,
    phone, and service-area as the site. Add photos, hours, and service
    categories (Painter, House Cleaning Service). Ask happy customers for
    reviews here — reviews are the #1 local ranking and trust factor.
11. **Bing Places** (quick, free, mirrors Google) and **Apple Business
    Connect** (so the business shows in Apple Maps).
12. **Consistent NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) everywhere — Google, the website,
    Facebook, any directory. Inconsistency hurts local search.

## Phase E — Domain & website go-live

13. **Register the domain.** Per your earlier research, **Cloudflare Registrar**
    or **Porkbun** (~$10/yr, free WHOIS privacy, no renewal price spikes).
    Buy `DustinYelton.com`; consider grabbing `YeltonPaintingNKY.com` or
    similar as a cheap services-focused redirect.
14. **Connect the domain to the live site** (Netlify makes this a few clicks:
    *Domain management → Add a domain* → follow the DNS steps, free HTTPS
    included).
15. **Swap the placeholders** on the site (phone, email, photos, real
    testimonial, bio, license/insurance/EPA language — only once true).
16. **Wire the contact form** to email Dustin (Netlify Forms is built in and
    free for low volume — just add `netlify` to the form tag; or Formspree).
17. **Launch + tell people.** Announce on personal social, hand out the URL,
    put it on a vehicle magnet / business cards / invoices.

## Phase F — First 90 days (momentum)

18. Collect **3–5 Google reviews** from the first jobs — ask in person and
    follow up with a text containing the review link.
19. Photograph **every** completed job (before/after) — this is the website's
    fuel and free social content.
20. Revisit **phase two** (music & art sections) only once services are
    steady — per the original plan, don't split focus too early.
